Rooted in tradition, powered by technology

From Ajoo in Ghana to Akawo in Nigeria and Stokvel in South Africa, rotational savings communities fuel Africa’s resilience. SaveHub was built to honour that trust, digitising what already works while removing the risk of a single point of failure.

Why now

The pain points that communities asked us to solve

Lack of trust and ledger transparency

Funds sit with one person. Records live in notebooks. When life happens, years of savings vanish.

Geography limits growth

Members abroad struggle to participate. Cash collections exclude remote and diaspora members.

Manual collections drain time

Leaders chase contributions, reconcile payments, and resolve disputes without digital tools.

Compliance uncertainty

KYC, AML, and regulatory requirements are complex, making scale risky for informal groups.

Platform pillars

What SaveHub delivers from day one

Hives of Five

Automated rotational payouts within curated groups of five to ensure intimacy, accountability, and reliability.

Trust Fabric

Multi-factor KYC, contribution history, and escrow-backed payouts eliminate single points of failure.

Payments Rail

Hooks into Flutterwave, Paystack, M-Pesa, and MTN MoMo with instant reconciliation and smart reminders.

Community Rewards

Earn points for streaks, referrals, and impact projects. Climb leaderboards and redeem with partner perks.

How it works

From onboarding to first payout in five steps

  1. 01

    Create your verified profile

    Complete tiered KYC with ID, biometrics-ready options, and add beneficiaries for peace of mind.

  2. 02

    Join or launch a Hive

    Use intelligent matching or invite your circle. Configure cadence (daily, weekly, monthly) and contribution amount.

  3. 03

    Automate collections

    SaveHub handles payment pulls, cross-border settlements, and nudges to keep everyone on track.

  4. 04

    Celebrate payouts

    Payouts land in verified wallets with escrow protections and instant notifications to the group.

  5. 05

    Grow your reputation

    Earn badges, stack points, unlock larger circles, and access credit products built on your savings history.
